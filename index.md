
# LiftBasic  
Your simple, clean companion for tracking workouts, body weight, hydration, and daily progress.

LiftBasic is designed to help you stay consistent without distractions.  
Track your training, monitor your weight trends, log your water intake, and keep your fitness journey organised — all in one clean, native iOS experience.

---

### Key Capabilities

The app offers five primary features:

1. **Workout Logging**: Users can record sets, reps, weights, and notes via a streamlined interface
2. **Body Weight Tracking**: Includes trend visualization, target setting, and progress analysis with charts
3. **Step Tracking**: Reads your daily step count from Apple Health and displays it in a calendar view
4. **Hydration Tracking**: Water intake logging using quick-add buttons or manual entry
5. **Daily Overview**: Quick access to essential statistics through TodayView
