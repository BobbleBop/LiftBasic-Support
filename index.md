
# LiftBasic  
Your simple, clean companion for tracking workouts, body weight, hydration, and daily progress.

LiftBasic is designed to help you stay consistent without distractions.  
Track your training, monitor your weight trends, log your water intake, and keep your fitness journey organised — all in one clean, native iOS experience.

---

## Features

- **Workout Logging**  
  Record sets, reps, weights, and notes with a clean, fast interface.

- **Body Weight Tracking**  
  View trends, set targets, and analyse progress with clear charts.

- **Hydration Tracking**  
  Log water intake quickly with chevron controls or manual entries.

- **Daily Overview**  
  See your key stats at a glance with TodayView.
